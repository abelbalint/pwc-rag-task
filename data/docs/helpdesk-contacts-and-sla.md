# Helpdesk Contacts and SLA

This document lists how to reach the Acme Corp IT helpdesk and what response times to expect.

## Contact channels

| Channel | Details |
|---|---|
| Portal | `https://helpdesk.acme.example` |
| Phone | +1 555 010 0100 |
| Email | `helpdesk@acme.example` |
| Walk-in | IT desk, ground floor, Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 16:00 |

The portal is the preferred channel because it records your request and lets you follow its progress.

## Support hours

The helpdesk is staffed Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 Central European Time, except public holidays. Outside these hours, only critical incidents are handled through the on-call line at +1 555 010 0199.

## Priorities and response times

| Priority | Definition | First response | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 Critical | Company-wide outage, or a security incident | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 High | A team cannot work, or a single user is fully blocked without a workaround | 1 hour | 1 business day |
| P3 Normal | A single user has a problem but can work around it | 4 business hours | 3 business days |
| P4 Low | Question or minor request | 1 business day | 5 business days |

Response and resolution times are counted only during support hours, except for P1.

## Writing a good ticket

Include the following to speed up the resolution:

- A short description of the problem and what you expected to happen.
- The exact error message, or a screenshot.
- Your device name, shown on the sticker on the laptop.
- When the problem started and whether anything changed before it.

## Escalation

If your ticket has not received a response within the times above, reply to the ticket email with the word "Escalate", or call the helpdesk. Unresolved P1 and P2 tickets are escalated automatically to the IT manager.

## Feedback

After a ticket is closed you receive a short survey. The helpdesk targets a satisfaction score of at least 90 percent.
