# Data Security Basics

These rules apply to every Acme Corp employee and contractor.

## Data classification

| Class | Examples | Handling |
|---|---|---|
| Public | Marketing material, job postings | No restrictions |
| Internal | Intranet pages, internal announcements | Share only within Acme |
| Confidential | Customer data, contracts, financial reports | Share only with authorized people, encrypted |
| Restricted | Passwords, encryption keys, personal HR records | Never share by email or chat |

## Protecting your device

- Lock your screen when you leave your desk. The shortcut is Windows key + L.
- The screen locks automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
- Full-disk encryption is enabled on all company laptops. Do not disable it.
- Do not connect unknown USB drives. Removable media are blocked by default.

## Email and phishing

- Be suspicious of unexpected attachments, urgent payment requests, and links that ask for your password.
- IT will never ask for your password by email, phone, or chat.
- Report a suspicious email with the "Report phishing" button in Outlook, or forward it to `phishing@acme.example`.
- If you clicked a link or opened an attachment by mistake, call the helpdesk immediately at +1 555 010 0100.

## Sharing files

- Share files through the company OneDrive or SharePoint, never through personal accounts or consumer file-sharing sites.
- Use expiring links and share with named people rather than "anyone with the link".
- Confidential files must not be sent as unencrypted email attachments.

## Remote work

- Use the company VPN on public or untrusted networks.
- Do not let family members or friends use your company laptop.
- Avoid working with confidential data in public places where a screen can be seen.

## Reporting a security incident

Report any lost device, suspected account compromise, or data leak at once. Call +1 555 010 0100 or write to `security@acme.example`. Reporting quickly matters more than being sure; you will not be penalized for a report made in good faith.
