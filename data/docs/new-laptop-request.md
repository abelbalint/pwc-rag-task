# New Laptop Request

This guide explains how employees request a new or replacement laptop.

## Who is eligible

- New hires receive a laptop before their first working day.
- Employees are eligible for a replacement every 4 years.
- A laptop that is broken, stolen, or no longer meets job requirements can be replaced earlier with manager approval.

## Standard models

| Profile | Model | Typical users |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Acme Book 14, 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD | Office, sales, HR |
| Performance | Acme Book Pro 16, 32 GB RAM, 1 TB SSD | Developers, designers |
| Ultralight | Acme Book Air 13, 16 GB RAM, 256 GB SSD | Frequent travellers |

Non-standard hardware requires written justification and approval from the IT manager.

## How to request

1. Open `https://helpdesk.acme.example` and choose Request > Hardware > Laptop.
2. Select the profile and enter the reason for the request.
3. Your manager receives an approval request by email and must approve it within 3 business days.
4. After approval, IT prepares the laptop.

## Delivery times

- Standard and Ultralight models are delivered within 5 business days after approval.
- Performance models are delivered within 10 business days after approval.
- Collection is at the IT desk on the ground floor, open Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 16:00. Bring your employee badge.

## Lost or stolen laptops

Report a lost or stolen laptop to the helpdesk immediately at +1 555 010 0100, and in any case within 2 hours of discovering the loss. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device.

## Returning the old laptop

Return the old laptop to the IT desk within 14 days of receiving the new one. Do not wipe the device yourself; IT securely erases it.
