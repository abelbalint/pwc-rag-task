# Printing

This guide explains how to print, scan, and fix common printer problems at Acme Corp.

## Secure printing

Acme uses follow-me printing. A print job is stored on the print server and released only when you badge in at a printer.

1. Print to the queue named `Acme-Secure` from any application.
2. Go to any Acme printer and tap your employee badge on the reader.
3. Select your job on the touchscreen and press Print.

Jobs that are not released are deleted automatically after 24 hours.

## Adding the printer queue

On company laptops the `Acme-Secure` queue is installed automatically. If it is missing, open the Software Center and install "Acme Print Queue".

## Scanning

Every multifunction printer can scan to your email. Badge in, choose Scan to Email, and the document arrives within a minute as a PDF. The maximum size is 20 MB.

## Colour and double-sided printing

- Printing is black and white and double-sided by default.
- Colour printing is available on the printers marked "Colour" on floors 2 and 4. Use colour only when necessary.
- Large print jobs over 100 pages should be sent to the print room on the ground floor.

## Troubleshooting

### Job does not appear on the printer

Wait 1 minute, and check that you printed to `Acme-Secure`. Make sure you badged in on a network-connected printer.

### Paper jam or empty toner

Follow the instructions on the printer screen. For toner replacement or a jam that you cannot clear, open a ticket at `https://helpdesk.acme.example` and note the printer ID printed on the label at the front.

### Badge not recognized

Register your badge at the helpdesk. Registration is done once, and it takes effect immediately.
